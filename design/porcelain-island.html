Porcelain Island

A redesign proposal for the Dynamic Island, and a working one. The island is a glazed object that hangs from the screen edge, with Astra's eyes as its only colour at rest. Add to-dos, deadlines, events, notes, friends and files, run timers, chat with Astra and start calls. Everything you add is saved in this browser. Click the island to open the card, hover it to peek.

Animation

Every pill, side by side

Sizes are the ones the island springs to, in layout pixels before the Size setting scales them. Widths that follow content are shown at a typical value.

Motion

One spring drives the outline, the ears, the timer bubble and the rail highlight, so everything that moves together arrives together. Each curve below is simulated from the same stiffness, damping and mass the Animation setting stores.

Content cross-fade

Old content leaves in 80 ms with a 6 px blur. New content waits 100 ms for the outline to start moving, then sharpens in over 220 ms from 96% scale.

Timer bubble

The bubble is its own blob in the goo layer. It grows from 40% scale and slides out past the right ear, so it visibly pinches off the pill instead of appearing next to it.

Astra's glow

Listening breathes with the microphone level, thinking orbits as a conic sweep, speaking holds steady. A ringing call borrows the same halo in green.

Page switches

Moving right through the rail slides the page in from the right, moving left slides it from the left. The highlight under the rail springs to the new group.
