The island, redrawn

A wider, shorter card with the section tabs along the bottom edge, plus every state the pill goes through, animated with the app's own spring settings. Hover the pill to peek, click it to open, and use the tabs to move around.

Design direction follows Anthropic's frontend-design skill from the anthropics/skills repository.

Hover the island to peek. Click it to open.

What changes everywhere

  • Wide and short. The open card is 760 by 300 instead of a tall column, so it covers less of what you're working on and pages lay out side by side.
  • Tabs at the bottom. Eight labelled icons sit on the card's lower edge, closest to your pointer after it drops down. A soft highlight slides between them.
  • Sub-pages at the top right. Sections with more than one page use one small switch beside the title, in the same place every time.
  • A status line in the header. Each page says its state in a few words, like "3 due today" or "2 online".

Every state of the pill

Each demo plays on a loop. The spring switch uses the three presets from your settings file, so you can compare how bouncy, smooth and snappy feel.

Pill sizes come from the shape table in App.tsx (idle 34 high, peek 40, alerts 40 to 56, incoming call 64). The card size and the Astra pill sizes are proposals.
