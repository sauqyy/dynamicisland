Dynamic Island · design proposal · v0.9 concept

Halo, the island with a lit edge

Everything the island does today, redrawn around one idea. The island is a plain ink shape, and a thin line of light along its rim tells you what it's doing. Downloads fill the rim. Calls make it pulse. Astra makes it breathe. Music tints it to the album colour. Inside the open card, the eight sections move to a rail on the left, so the card is shorter and the page has more room.
