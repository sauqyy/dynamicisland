✦ ✦What lives in the island

Small space.
Big personality.

It stays out of your way until you need it, then melts open with exactly the right controls.

01 / Now playing

Every song gets the main stage.

Album art, transport controls and time-synced lyrics from LRCLIB, right where you're already looking.

  • Click any lyric to jump to it
  • Three animated equalizer styles
  • Colors adapt to the album art
02 / Focus

Time that breaks free.

Start a timer, stopwatch, or Pomodoro. The active timer stretches from the island and splits into its own gooey bubble, always visible without getting in the way.

  • Timer with draining progress
  • Stopwatch with lap tracking
  • Built-in Pomodoro sessions
03 / System alerts

Useful updates. Zero interruption.

Volume, brightness, battery, charging and downloads appear for a moment, show their progress, then quietly fold back into the pill.

04 / Together

Your people are one click away.

See who is online, chat one-to-one or in groups, and take voice calls with up to six people. Incoming calls ring right inside the island.

  • Friends, groups, and read receipts
  • One-to-one and group voice calls
